**Implements:** follow-up to `07. Discovery, Pairing & Config Sync.spec.md`
— cosmetic only, no protocol or state-machine change.
**TL;DR:** replace the QR scanner's plain full-screen camera preview with a
real viewfinder — a focused rectangle, dimmed/blurred surroundings, and
status copy moved into that dimmed area instead of floating over the live
camera feed.

## Scope

**In:**
1. A viewfinder overlay on `PairingView.scannerView` (`ios/Buttons/Views/PairingView.swift`):
   a centered focus rectangle, with the area outside it dimmed and/or
   blurred (bokeh) — exact treatment (dim vs. blur vs. both) is an open
   question for whoever picks this up, not decided here.
2. Move the scan instruction text into the dimmed/blurred surrounding area
   — centered on screen, not overlaid on the live camera feed — for
   discoverability (where attention already goes) and contrast/legibility
   against a dimmed background instead of an unpredictable camera image.

**Out:**
1. Any change to `DataScannerViewController`'s actual scan behavior,
   recognized symbologies, or the `onScan` callback contract.
2. Any change to the camera-permission states (`cameraPreAsk`,
   `cameraDenied`) or `landingView`/`failedView` — this is scoped to the
   `.scanning` phase's visual only.
3. Deciding dim vs. blur vs. both — left to implementation; note the
   decision in this file's Implementation Notes once made.

> **Rule for agent:** stay inside this scope. If something out of scope
> blocks you, stop and flag it — don't silently expand scope to route
> around it.

## Files to Touch

1. `ios/Buttons/Views/PairingView.swift` — modify — `scannerView`, plus a
   new overlay view (inline or extracted, implementer's call).

## Definition of Done

1. [ ] `.scanning` phase shows a centered focus rectangle over the live
       camera preview, with the surrounding area dimmed and/or blurred —
       not a plain full-screen unmasked camera feed.
2. [ ] Scan instruction copy sits in the dimmed/blurred area, not
       floating on the raw camera image.
3. [ ] Scanning a real QR code still works — the overlay is visual only,
       doesn't change `DataScannerViewController`'s recognition region or
       the `onScan` callback.
